## Customer Retention & Churn Analysis
### Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics | Task 2

---

## 🔍 About the Task
This project analyzes customer retention and churn behavior using subscription data.
The goal is to understand why customers leave, identify high-risk segments, and recommend actions to improve retention.

---

## 📁 Dataset
**Telco Customer Churn Dataset**
- Customer demographics
- Contract type
- Monthly charges
- Internet & payment services
- Churn status

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Power BI (Dashboard & DAX)
- Microsoft Excel (Data cleaning)
- DAX Measures

---

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis
The dashboard answers key business questions:
- Overall churn rate and distribution
- Churn by contract type
- Churn by tenure group
- Churn by internet service
- Churn by payment method
- Churn rate by monthly charges range

---

## 🔑 Key Insights
- Month-to-month contracts have the highest churn
- High monthly charges increase churn risk
- Long-term contracts improve retention
- Fiber optic users churn more than DSL users

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Encourage long-term contracts through discounts
- Target high-charge customers with retention offers
- Improve service quality for fiber optic users
